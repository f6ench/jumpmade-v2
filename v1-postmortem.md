# V1 Post-Mortem

**Author:** Joseph French  
**Written:** 2026-04-21  
**V1 scope:** Jumpmade V1 / Sniper OS — roughly February 2026 through the split on 2026-04-21.

---

## Why this document exists

V2 cannot safely start until the founder has answered, in writing, what actually went wrong in V1. The V1 audit surfaced external patterns (missed revenue target, GHL cancellation, one stalled customer, drifted ICP) — this file is for the **internal** patterns that produced those externals. Claude can review this for gaps once it's drafted, but the content has to come from you alone. Do not let anyone — including Claude — draft the emotional content.

Write this in one sitting if you can. Do not polish it. Do not make it presentable. Nobody is reading this but you and future-you.

---

## Section 1 — What actually happened in the first week of March 2026

I was doing nothing — just procrastination, working on other side projects, dealing with some family issues, so everything with Jumpmade had become stagnant. I also felt like I wasn't getting anywhere with that current version of Jumpmade at the time. I lost wind because I didn't find anyone who had any burning desire to buy the product. Things were getting expensive. I couldn't close Alec because the tool kept on breaking down. I felt like I was over building. I was gradually losing belief in the project. I felt like I was spreading myself too thin.

---

## Section 2 — What V1 patterns were you defending against awareness of?

I was secretly using building the tool as a disguise to avoid selling. I was over iterating for one customer. I was pursuing warm intros as a method for converting but was too hell-bent on using cold SMS as my outreach strategy when it clearly wasn't working. I was drifting horizontally due to not believing in my product. I had no real external accountability. The iteration of the product continually breaking down resulted in me constantly iterating. I wasn't believing my offer.

---

## Section 3 — What did you want V1 to be that it wasn't?

I wanted V1 to be the best speed-to-lead revenue generator for plumbers out there. The drift happened when I wasn't seeing good response to the only outreach method I was relying on at the time — cold SMS outreach. For that reason I didn't know whether my outreach method was bad or my product/offer was bad.

---

## Section 4 — What would you have told a friend in your exact position on 2026-03-15?

Sell first, build second. Give the market what it wants. Distribution is key. Proof of demand is vital before building and scaling. Fail fast.

---

## Section 5 — The honest list

All of the above — interrupting people to sell to them, having conversations, getting over social anxiety to pitch to people.

I failed to have uncomfortable conversations with clients, even when I knew they were unsatisfied and I had already delivered on my end. I think also that it was because I failed to clearly outline what I was delivering before the agreement, which meant there was always grey area over whether I had delivered exactly what the opposing party wanted to a T.

I stopped looking at the fees I was burning through in sending cold SMS texts, the sunk costs for the GHL subscription, and the running GHL costs for keeping Alec's sub-account open.

I really did prefer building the tool because it felt productive.

I didn't ask any of my entrepreneur friends for advice on the direction of the project — friends who run big-scale agencies. I found it difficult to ask them for help. I didn't ask my tradesman family member to refer me to plumbers to test the market with them.

---

## Section 6 — What must be true about V2 for this not to repeat?

Someone who is solely focused on booking meetings, getting in front of prospects, and filling the pipeline. Sell what people want. Don't over-complicate things. Stay in one vertical and master it before even letting drift horizontally be a thought. Keep costs as low as possible.

---

## Section 7 — What are you still unwilling to commit to?

I think 12 weeks is too long as a period to refuse to re-open focus. I think it should be 4 weeks MAX. I think that's enough time to get pedal to the metal with filling out the pipeline and getting on calls to find a proof of concept.

---

## Section 8 — What do you want future-you to know?

You need to Sell, Sell, Sell. Main focus should be filling the pipeline with meetings and then building second. There is no point building any fancy moving parts if you aren't bringing in any revenue. If you stay committed and focused and don't sweat the small stuff you'll make it. Don't deviate from the main goal. Stay focused.
