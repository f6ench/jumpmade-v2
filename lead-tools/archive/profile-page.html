Hello, my name is Mark and welcome to M.L. Blakeman your local Plumber based in Didcot, Oxfordshire.

I am an experienced Gas safe and OFTEC (Oil) Plumber who always provides a professional and efficient service in all aspects of plumbing. We offer a wide variety of services ranging from advice and support over the phone, right the way through to full domestic installations. We specialise in bathroom installation, Boiler installation and pressurised system installation and servicing along with all your other plumbing needs. No job is too SMALL! I'm also proud to offer a 24/7 emergency call out.

I pride myself on working with you as the customer ensuring I see to your every need.

We cover all OX postcodes and other surrounding counties such as Berkshire and Buckinghamshire.

Look forward to hearing from you.
