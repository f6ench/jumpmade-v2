**Revenue / Financials:**

- Total assets: 3.75M (Dec 2024, up 135% YoY)

**Trustpilot:** 4.8/5 from 1,159 reviews. 94% 5-star. 4% 1-star.

**Pricing:** Not published. Discovery Day (free/low-cost) -> sales call -> academy enrolment. Likely 2,000-5,000+ per programme.

### SWOT

**Strengths:**
- Celebrity founder (Apprentice winner = instant credibility with tradespeople)
- Massive scale -- 49 employees, 1M/month revenue, 1,159 Trustpilot reviews
- Proven growth trajectory (100 -> 3.75M assets in 4 years)
- Discovery Day events create in-person trust before the sale
- Strong content marketing (podcast, YouTube, press coverage in Peterborough Today)
- Adding done-for-you marketing services (expanding from coaching-only)
- "Free marketing audit worth 500" lead magnet with urgency scarcity

**Weaknesses:**
- They teach, they don't build -- clients pay thousands and still need to implement
- High leverage (2.75M liabilities vs 1.01M net assets, 73% debt ratio)
- Cash position declining (157K, down 51%) while growing aggressively
- Pricing hidden -- suggests high-pressure sales process
- No technology product -- pure services business, hard to scale beyond headcount
- Generic coaching (covers all trades, not specialised)
- No AI, no automation, no lead sniping -- analog model

**Opportunities for Jumpmade:**
- Their graduates are our ideal prospects -- coached to value systems but haven't built them
- "We build what they teach" positioning
- Could partner: Trade Mastermind coaches the mindset, Jumpmade builds the system
- Their move into DFY marketing means they'll eventually need to offer lead gen tools -- we could white-label

**Threats from them:**
- If they build their own tech product, they have the audience to dominate
- Their DFY marketing arm is creeping toward our space
- Brand recognition -- Joseph Valente is a known name in UK trades

### Pros (from their customer's perspective)
- Celebrity credibility and social proof
- In-person events build real trust
- Comprehensive business education (not just marketing)
- Large community of other tradespeople

### Cons (from their customer's perspective)
- Expensive (likely 2-5K+) with no tangible product
- Still need to do the implementation yourself
- High-pressure sales after Discovery Day
- Teaching model doesn't guarantee results

## Key Insights for Offer 2.0

1. **The market is more crowded than we thought.** Trademate, Voice Install, FutureFlow, Bubyli -- multiple players building AI + automation for service businesses. But none have won yet. All are pre-revenue or very early.

2. **Trade Mastermind proves tradespeople will pay thousands for business help.** 10M+ revenue selling coaching at 2-5K. Our 1,500 + 300/mo is a fraction of that -- and we actually build the system.

3. **Checkatrade TradeMore is the platform-level threat.** Free AI job management from the platform that owns the leads. We MUST position as platform-agnostic and focus on what TradeMore doesn't do: AI response speed, multi-platform, voice AI, follow-up automation.

4. **Nobody has proof.** Voice Install: zero testimonials. FutureFlow: zero. Trademate: zero. Bubyli: unknown. CallChimps: zero. The first company to get 5-10 real case studies with numbers wins the trust race.

5. **The coaching graduates are still our best prospects.** Tradespeople who've paid 2-5K for coaching already value systems and growth. They just need someone to build it. Trade Mastermind creates 1,000+ of these per year.

6. **We need a named mechanism.** Voice Install has "Strike Window." We need our own. "The Revenue Machine" is a working name but needs refinement.

7. **Live demos convert.** Voice Install has "Try It Yourself." Plumber's Mate AI has a voice widget. CallChimps has an audio demo. We must have a demo experience on our landing page.

8. **Our pricing is validated.** Bubyli charges 1,497 + 297/mo. Voice Install charges $500 + $299/mo. Our 1,500 + 300/mo sits right in the market. Launch pricing at 750 + 300/mo is competitive.

9. **Postcode exclusivity is a differentiator nobody else uses** (except Plumbers Marketing Co's one-per-city model). This is our strongest urgency and retention mechanism.

10. **Our real competition is still inertia.** Most tradespeople aren't buying from anyone. All these competitors combined serve fewer than a few hundred clients. There are 300,000+ trade businesses in the UK.
