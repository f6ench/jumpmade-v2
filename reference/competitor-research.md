# Competitor Research

**Last Updated:** 17 Mar 2026
**Method:** Website teardowns (Playwright), Companies House API, web search

---

## Landscape Summary

Five categories of competitor:

1. **Trades business coaches** -- high-ticket coaching/consulting. They teach, they don't build. 1,500-5,000+ for courses, 300-1,000+/mo ongoing.
2. **Lead gen agencies** -- generate leads via SEO/ads/directories. Charge per lead or revenue share. No workflow automation.
3. **Trades CRM / software** -- SaaS tools for job management, invoicing, quoting. No leads, no AI.
4. **AI-powered done-for-you** -- closest to Jumpmade. AI receptionists, automation, done-for-you setup.
5. **AI call answering services** -- AI virtual receptionists sold as SaaS. No lead gen, no CRM setup.

6. **DFY marketing automation** -- done-for-you marketing + CRM setup for trades. GHL-based. Setup fee + monthly retainer.

Jumpmade's position (post-pivot): we AUDIT the business, BUILD the AI-powered revenue system (CRM, AI response, voice AI, follow-up, review engine), and MAINTAIN it monthly. Platform-agnostic -- not tied to Checkatrade. Lead sniping is an upsell, not the core.
